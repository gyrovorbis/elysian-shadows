    The Elysian Shadows Toolkit or "ESTk" is a multiplatform game development environment facilitating the creation of the "next generation" of 2D games. Originally developed solely to power Elysian Shadows, it features a full-fledged dynamic lighting engine, pixel perfect shadows, 3D positional audio, 3D rigid body and particle physics, and is fully extensible through an extremely powerful Lua API.
